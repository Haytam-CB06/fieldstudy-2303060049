## What is working well

**Clear search recovery.** Events explains when a search finds nothing and suggests adjusting the criteria.

**Visible loading feedback.** Coach profiles show placeholders while their details load, so visitors can see that content is on its way. Both are useful touches to keep.

## Three things to fix first

**1. Make finding a session work without a mouse.**

The keyboard test could open Events, but it skipped the sport shortcuts that worked with a mouse. The location, sport and language filters also lacked names explaining their purpose to reading software. Some visitors may struggle to reach a suitable session or leave before finding one. Ask your developer to make the shortcuts work from the keyboard and give every filter a clear, permanent label.

**Check:** use Tab to reach Yoga and press Enter. It should open the same results as a mouse click. Ask the developer to demonstrate that reading software announces each filter's purpose as well as its selected value.

**2. Make the lighter text easier to read.**

The browser checks flagged weak text/background contrast on the home and Events pages. People with low vision or using the site in bright light may miss details they need to choose a session. Ask your developer to identify the flagged text and adjust its colours, including when a control is selected or focused.

**Check:** revisit the affected text on both pages and repeat the same browser accessibility check. The contrast warnings for those elements should be gone; a higher overall score alone is not enough.

**3. Keep the helpful missing-page screen, but correct its response.**

An invented address shows a useful missing-page message and home link, but the website reports success behind the scenes. That can confuse search engines and broken-link checks. Ask your developer to send a proper "not found" response while preserving the recovery page.

**Check:** open /no-such-page-123 and use its home link. The developer should also show that this address returns "404 - not found", rather than a success response.

The order puts access to the main visitor task first, followed by readability and then missing-page handling. Thank you for making coaches and group activities easier to discover.
