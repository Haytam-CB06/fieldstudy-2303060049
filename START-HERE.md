# How to complete this repository
**Focus:** use this as your work plan; it is not part of your report.

Replace every `[FILL: ...]` with your own work. Delete guidance blocks from final PDFs. Never replace a missing observation with a guess. Record "not observed", "not applicable" with a reason, or "not tested safely" when accurate.

## What you personally do
- Claim the site, browse it, use DevTools, take original screenshots, run Lighthouse, and interpret your evidence.
- Choose your own three priorities from what you actually observed.
- If attempting S2, recruit one non-computing volunteer and personally observe them.
- AI may explain concepts or tidy English; disclose use. Do not present generated findings as fieldwork.

## A practical sequence
1. Confirm the site meets eligibility rules: public pages, multiple pages, search/form, named organisation, and genuine relevance to you. Do not log in.
2. Fill the README metadata. Confirm the claim deadline: the document mentions both Week 4 and Week 5.
3. Start a fresh browser window and record browser/version, date, time and timezone.
4. Complete A1-A7, then B1-B8 using the fieldwork guide. Save evidence immediately.
5. Fill the report using observed facts, then explain what those facts mean for visitors.
6. Select two strengths and three priority problems backed by evidence.
7. Fill the owner summary in plain language.
8. Attempt optional tasks only after Core is complete.
9. Export, inspect and commit the final PDFs; merge to main and submit the repository URL.

## Suggested genuine commits over three or more days
- Day 1: finish technical observations and attach evidence.
- Day 2: finish usability/accessibility observations and attach evidence.
- Day 3: write evidence-backed recommendations and owner summary.
- Day 3 or later: export PDFs and complete final review.
Choose messages describing work actually completed. These are suggestions, not dates to manufacture.

## Formatting plan
Aim for pages 1-2: Part A; pages 3-4: Part B; pages 5-6: Part C. Use a compact title block on page 1. Put the evidence-log appendix after those pages. Do not force long tables into tiny text.
